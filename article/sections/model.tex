\section{Mathematical Model}

Let \(D\subset\mathbb{R}^d\), \(d=1,2,3\), be a bounded domain with Lipschitz boundary \(\partial D\). 
Let \(T>0\) be a fixed final time.

We consider a complete probability space

\[
(\Omega,\mathcal{F},\mathbb{P})
\]

equipped with a filtration

\[
{\mathcal{F}*t}*{t\in[0,T]}
\]

satisfying the usual conditions.

Let

\[
W(t)
\]

be a one-dimensional Wiener process defined on this probability space.

The state variable

\[
y=y(x,t,\omega)
\]

represents the concentration of a transported quantity, such as a pollutant, temperature, or chemical species.

The control variable

\[
u=u(x,t)
\]

acts as a distributed source term.

The stochastic advection--diffusion equation under consideration is

\[
dy
+
\left(
\mathbf{b}\cdot\nabla y
-
\kappa \Delta y
\right)dt
u\,dt
+
\sigma\, dW(t),
\qquad
(x,t)\in D\times(0,T),
\]

where

\begin{itemize}
\item \(\mathbf{b}(x)\in\mathbb{R}^d\) denotes a prescribed velocity field,
\item \(\kappa>0\) is the diffusion coefficient,
\item \(u\) is the control function,
\item \(\sigma\ge 0\) is the noise intensity.
\end{itemize}

The equation is supplemented with homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions

\[
y=0,
\qquad
(x,t)\in \partial D\times(0,T),
\]

and the initial condition

\[
y(x,0)=y_0(x),
\qquad
x\in D.
\]

Throughout this work, we assume that

\[
y_0\in L^2(\Omega;L^2(D)).
\]

The stochastic forcing term models uncertainties arising from environmental fluctuations, unresolved physical processes, or measurement errors.

For convenience, we rewrite the state equation in the abstract form

\[
dy + Ay,dt = Bu,dt + \sigma,dW(t),
\]

where the differential operator

\[
A:H_0^1(D)\rightarrow H^{-1}(D)
\]

is defined by

\[
Ay
=
-\kappa \Delta y
+
\mathbf{b}\cdot\nabla y,
\]

and

\[
B=I
\]

is the identity control operator.

The analysis developed in the following sections will rely on the variational formulation of this 
stochastic evolution equation.
